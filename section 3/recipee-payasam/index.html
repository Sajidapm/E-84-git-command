Ada pradhaman | Payasam

Published on 2,2026 by Rekha

Ingradients you need

  • Ada – ¾ cup
  • Full cream milk (boiled) – 1 litre
  • Milk maid – ½ can or as per taste
  • Caster sugar – ¼ cup
  • Green Cardamom – 4 pods
  • Nutmeg powder – ¼ teaspoon
  • Ghee – 2 tablespoons
  • Cashew nut – ¼ cup
  • Raisins – ¼ cup

How to make it

  1. In a pan bring water to rolling boil, turn the heat off and add the ada, cover and let it sit for 20-30 minutes. Or alternatively you could cook the ada as per the packet instructions. Strain and set aside.
  2. After you add the milk, add the cardamom and stir well. Add the milk maid and still well. Add the cooked ada and continue to cook till the payasam starts to thicken. Turn the heat off.
  3. Now in a small pan, heat the ghee and fry the cashew and raisins and add to the payasam. Serve warm or cold, they are delicious either way!
